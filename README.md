# S.M.V.

**Shrish Mudumby Venugopal.** Autonomous agents, quantitative finance, computational law. Three frontiers, equal weight, one engineering standard.

I build the systems and the instruments behind them, from a first-place multi-agent
medical-billing advocate at MHacks 2026 to an MCP-native autonomous trading agent and
an SSRN paper proving most backtested edges are overfitting artifacts.

## Highlights

- **Amyna.** 1st Place, Relay Interactive Agents Track, MHacks 2026. A multi-agent system that
  helps patients dispute faulty medical bills. I built the backend, the Fetch.ai uAgents
  orchestration, and the 300-test suite.
- **MCP Trading Agent.** An Anthropic-only, MCP-native autonomous equity trading agent with
  hard risk rules enforced deterministically in code, plus a ModernBERT sentiment layer.
- **CRA Outstanding Undergraduate Researcher Award nominee (2026).**
- **CourtListener PR #6703.** Merged into the Free Law Project’s production archive, about a
  65× tokenizer speedup (0.22s to 0.003s).
- **Empirical Evaluation of Cross-Sectional Equity Signals.** Published on SSRN, PBO near 0.60.
- **Politi-folio.** 1st Place, BNY Track, TartanHacks 2026. XRP Ledger reconciliation and risk
  monitoring for back-office fintech.
